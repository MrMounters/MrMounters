Behind the Studio

Behind Plume & Pixxel

Bonjour, I'm Jasmine H. — a creative studio founder from New York and Dakar, Senegal. Born at the intersection of French and Senegalese culture, I've channeled that duality into design, creativity, and strategy.

Plume & Pixxel was born from the desire to create a studio where beauty and performance aren't opposites — a site that looks incredible AND converts clients.

  • Between NYC & Dakar — fresh eyes on every new market
  • Bilingual — English & French 🇫🇷
  • Rapid turnaround — most sites delivered in 5–7 days
  • Flexible payment — in USD, Euros, or CFA
  • Real partnership — we stay engaged, not just deliverers
