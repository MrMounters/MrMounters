## Copy rules (keep it converting & compliant)
- Hook in the first line — 80% of performance is the first 3 seconds / first line.
- One idea per ad. Benefit first, mechanism second.
- Plain words. Short sentences. Talk like a person, not a brochure.
- **Never** invent stats, reviews, or medical/earnings outcomes. Use real, client-approved proof only.
- Always end on one clear CTA: **Book a Free AI Audit.**
