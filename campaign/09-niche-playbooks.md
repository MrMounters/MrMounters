**Meta primary text — long:** Front desk swamped with patients in chairs? After-hours calls going to voicemail? ### 9. Home Remodeling / Contractors / Solar
High-ticket ($5k–$60k+ jobs), quote-request-heavy, homeowner shops 3–5 bids fast. Angle: "The contractor who calls back first usually wins the bid — our AI answers every quote request in seconds and books the estimate." Reuse Angle 3 (fire the busywork) and Angle 5 (show-don't-tell demo) hardest — visual "watch it book a DM" content performs well here. `utm_campaign`: `salon_[city]_busywork`, `lashstudio_[city]_demo`.
