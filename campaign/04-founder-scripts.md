# Founder Video Scripts (John, on camera)

**Format:** 15–45s vertical (9:16), phone or camera. Hook in the first 3 seconds. Talk to one person. B-roll notes in *(italics)*. End on one CTA: **book a free AI audit.** Keep captions on (85% watch muted).

---

## Script 1 — Missed Money (20–25s)
**Hook (0–3s, look into lens):** "Your [med spa] is losing appointments right now — and you can't even see it happening."
**Problem (3–10s):** "Every call your front desk misses, every DM you answer six hours late — that person just booked with the [med spa] down the street."
**Mechanism (10–18s):** "I build AI agents that answer, qualify, and book every lead in seconds. 24/7. Done for you." *(cut to phone: AI texting a lead back + calendar filling)*
**CTA (18–25s):** "Book a free AI audit — I'll show you exactly how many leads you're leaking. Link below."

## Script 2 — Speed (15–20s)
**Hook:** "The [med spa] that replies first almost always wins the client."
**Body:** "Most places take hours. Our AI replies in seconds — answers questions, books the [appointment], done." *(B-roll: 3-second timer, notification, booked)*
**CTA:** "Free AI audit — see your response-time gap. Link below."

## Script 3 — Fire the Busywork (25–30s)
**Hook:** "You don't need to fire anyone to add AI. You need to fire the busywork."
**Body:** "Your team didn't get into this to chase no-shows and answer the same ten questions all day. The AI takes the repetitive 80% — instant replies, booking, reminders, follow-up — so your people focus on the clients in the chair."
**CTA:** "I'll build it for you, done-for-you. Free audit — link below."

## Script 4 — Founder Authority / Intro (30–40s)
**Hook:** "I'm John. I build AI front-desk agents for local [med spas] here in Scottsdale."
**Body:** "Not a chatbot gimmick — real agents that answer, qualify, and book every lead the second it comes in. My clients stop losing appointments to missed calls and slow replies, and they keep their whole team. We get it live in days, not months." *(B-roll: dashboard, phone thread)*
**Proof/guarantee:** "It's live in [X] days or you don't pay for month one."
**CTA:** "Grab a free AI audit and I'll show you where you're leaking [appointments]. Link below."

## Script 5 — Show-Don't-Tell Demo (25–35s)
**Hook:** "Let me just show you." *(screen-record starts)*
**Body:** "New lead comes in… watch. The AI replies in seconds, answers her question, checks the calendar, and books her — while I'm sitting here doing nothing." *(narrate the live text thread → booked)*
**CTA:** "This runs 24/7 for my clients. Want it for your [med spa]? Free audit — link below."

## Script 6 — FOMO / After Hours (20–25s)
**Hook:** "This came in at 11:47 last night." *(show a timestamped lead notification)*
**Body:** "Most [med spas] never see that revenue — it goes to voicemail till morning, and by then she's booked elsewhere. My AI booked her at 11:48." *(booked confirmation)*
**CTA:** "Your AI never clocks out. Free audit — link below."

## Script 7 — Objection Handling (30s, retargeting)
**Hook:** "'John, I already have a front desk.' Good — keep them."
**Body:** "The AI isn't replacing your team, it's covering the gaps they can't — after hours, when the phone's ringing off the hook, the 11pm form fill. It hands warm, booked clients to your people instead of losing them." 
**CTA:** "Free audit — see the gaps. Link below."

---

### Filming tips
- Shoot 3–5 hooks per script; the hook is 80% of performance — test them.
- First frame = your face + motion (walk in, sit down, hold the phone up). No slow intros.
- Natural light, eye-level, clean background (office / med-spa-adjacent).
- Keep energy up, pace fast, cut every dead beat.
- Always burn in captions.
