# Organic Social-Proof Calendar (IG · FB · LinkedIn)

**Goal:** build trust and warm the retargeting audience. 5–7 posts/week. Repurpose ad creative + founder clips. Every post = one idea + one soft CTA ("DM 'AUDIT'" or link in bio).

## Post-type rotation
| Type | Purpose | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Client win / result *(real only)* | Proof | Reel / carousel |
| Behind-the-scenes build | Authority | Reel / Story |
| "AI booked X while you were closed" | Desire | Reel |
| Myth-buster / education | Trust + reach | Carousel / talking-head |
| Founder POV / hot take | Personality | Talking-head Reel |
| Before/after (missed → booked) | Contrast | Static / carousel |
| Testimonial *(client-approved)* | Proof | Quote card / video |

## 2-week starter (repeatable)
**Week 1**
- Mon — Founder POV Reel: "Why local [med spas] lose 30% of leads (and don't know it)."
- Tue — Carousel (myth-buster): "5 places your [med spa] loses appointments."
- Wed — Behind-the-scenes: building/booking demo (screen record).
- Thu — Before/after static: Missed call ❌ → Booked by AI ✅.
- Fri — "AI never sleeps" Reel: after-hours lead booked at 11pm.
- Sat — Client win *(real)* or educational quote card.
- Sun — Founder story/values (why you built Meridion AI).

**Week 2**
- Mon — Talking-head: "Keep your team, fire the busywork."
- Tue — Carousel: "How the AI books an appointment, step by step."
- Wed — Testimonial *(client-approved)*.
- Thu — Speed demo: 3-second reply timer.
- Fri — FOMO Reel: "While you read this, a competitor's AI booked a client."
- Sat — Q&A / objection ("Is it a chatbot?" → no, here's the difference).
- Sun — Recap / soft CTA: "Taking [N] Scottsdale [med spas] this month."

## LinkedIn (2–3/week, founder-led)
- Educational carousel (document post) on lead leakage + AI fix.
- Personal build-in-public post (a client outcome, a lesson, a metric — real).
- Contrarian take on AI in local business.

## Rules
- Real proof only — no invented numbers or fake testimonials.
- Caption hook in line 1. Captions burned into video.
- One CTA per post. Batch-film 8–10 clips per session.
- Recycle winning ad creative into organic and vice-versa.
