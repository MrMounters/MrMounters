# Funnel Architecture & Channel Setup

## The funnel
```
COLD AD (Meta / Google / LinkedIn)
        │   utm_source / medium / campaign / content(creative id) on the link
        ▼
LANDING / short VSL  (ai-revenue-systems.html, or a dedicated ai-agents.html)
        │   primary CTA: "Book a Free AI Audit"
        ▼
CAL.COM booking (john-bert/15min)  ← UTMs passed through so the booking carries its source
        │   instant speed-to-lead follow-up (DOGFOOD the product)
        ▼
SALES CALL (show the AI live: text a lead back + book, on-screen)
        ▼
CLOSE → onboard → monthly ROI report → retain/expand
```

## Two entry points
1. **Book a call** (high intent) — primary CTA everywhere.
2. **Lead magnet** (not-ready-yet) — "Free AI Audit: see how many leads you're missing" or an "AI Readiness Scorecard." Captures the email, then nurtures to a call.

## Retargeting ladder (warm traffic)
1. Case study / result ("booked 11 appointments while she slept")
2. Objection handling ("Keep your team — fire the busywork")
3. Founder guarantee ("Live in X days or you don't pay")
4. Last-call / scarcity ("Taking 3 Scottsdale med spas this month")

## Channel setup
### Meta (primary demand-gen)
- Advantage+ / CBO. **Audiences:** (a) broad local geo + age/gender for med-spa owners; (b) interest stacks (med spa software, aesthetics business, small-business-owner, Booker/Boulevard/Vagaro); (c) lookalikes off leads → closers (later).
- **Placements:** Reels / Stories / Feed. **Objective:** Leads or Calls → Cal.com.
- Retarget: site visitors + 25%/50%/75% video viewers with the proof ladder.

### Google (intent capture)
- **Search** on high-intent terms (exact/phrase): "AI receptionist for med spa", "AI answering service dental", "missed call text back service", "automate lead follow up [niche]", "AI appointment booking [niche]".
- **PMax** for scale once conversion data exists. **Retargeting display** on the proof ladder. **Local/Call ads** where applicable.

### LinkedIn (Phase 3 — ABM/authority)
- Target job titles (owner, practice manager, marketing director) + industries + geo. Document/Conversation ads. Founder thought-leadership. Higher CPL, more qualified — best once the offer is proven.

## Tracking
GA4 + Meta Pixel/CAPI + Google conversion + existing Microsoft Clarity. **North-star conversion = "strategy call booked" (Cal.com).** UTMs flow all the way to the CRM so the in-app **Acquisition** panel ties spend → booked calls → closed clients (see plan §12b).
