# Meridion AI — AI Agents Campaign · Strategy

**Offer:** Done-for-you AI agents that run ~80% of a local business's front office — answer every lead in seconds, qualify, book the appointment, follow up, and reactivate old leads. **$5k–$10k/mo retainer, done-for-you.**

**One-liner:** *"AI agents that answer, qualify, and book every lead — so you never lose another customer to a missed call or a slow reply."*

**Big goal:** $100k/mo MRR at ~80% margin, fast. Founder-led (John on camera) + AI/UGC social proof. Start Scottsdale-local → national/worldwide.

---

## The wedge (why now, why this)
Local service businesses lose 20–40% of inbound to missed calls and slow follow-up. They *know* AI can fix it — they read about it daily — but they can't implement it and don't trust generic chatbots. We sell the outcome (booked appointments), not the tech. The founder-led angle beats faceless agencies on trust, which is the #1 blocker at this price point.

## Niche strategy — beachhead → expand
1. **Beachhead (Scottsdale, wks 1–8): Med spas & aesthetics.** $500–$3k/visit, female-skewed (fits UGC-with-women creative), Scottsdale-dense, missed call = lost high-margin appointment. Tight ICP → sharp creative → fast case studies → local authority.
2. **Wave 2 (national): Dental/ortho → home services (HVAC, roofing, plumbing).** Massive missed-call pain, high job value, national scale, less seasonal.
3. **Wave 3: Law (PI/family), real estate teams, derm/cosmetic.** Speed-to-lead = money → strongest ROI story.

**Play:** prove in one niche → template the funnel → clone per vertical (swap proof, headline, imagery). One engine, many skins.

## Positioning pillars
- **Speed:** answers in seconds, 24/7 — beats every competitor's voicemail.
- **Done-for-you:** they do nothing; we build, run, and optimize.
- **Keep your team:** AI eats the busywork (the 80%), humans do the high-value 20%.
- **Proof + guarantee:** live in X days or you don't pay; ROI framing (one recovered appointment pays for it).

## Channels (see 02-funnel + 07-measurement)
- **Meta** — primary demand-gen (founder + UGC creative, local then lookalike).
- **Google** — high-intent capture (search) + retargeting.
- **LinkedIn** — Phase 3 ABM to owners/practice managers.

## North-star metric
**Cost per booked strategy call** → then **CAC (cost per closed client)**. Everything optimizes toward booked calls that close.
