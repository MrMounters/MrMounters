# Offer & Economics — Path to $100k/mo at ~80% margin

> All figures are **planning models**, not promises. Validate against real numbers as data comes in.

## The offer stack
| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Core | AI front-office agents: instant lead response (SMS/chat/email), qualification, booking, follow-up sequences, dead-lead reactivation |
| Price | **$5k–$10k/mo** retainer (tiered by volume/channels/integrations). Blended target ~**$6.5k** |
| Setup | Optional one-time build/onboarding fee (e.g. $1.5k–$3k) — raises cash, filters tire-kickers |
| Guarantee | "Agents live in **X days** or you don't pay for month one" (risk reversal → higher close rate) |
| Done-for-you | We build, connect, run, and optimize. Client does nothing but approve |
| ROI frame | "One recovered appointment/job a month pays for it" |

## The $100k/mo math
- $100k/mo ÷ $6.5k blended = **~16 clients.** (Or ~12–13 at $8k.)
- **80% margin** → allowed COGS ≈ **$1.3k/client/mo** (AI infra + light human oversight + tools). Keep tooling lean to protect margin.
- **Client LTV:** ~8-mo avg retention × $6.5k ≈ **$52k** (before setup fees). → You can profitably pay **$3k–$6k CAC** and stay wildly positive. This is why the campaign can be aggressive.

## Funnel math to add ~4 clients/mo (≈ +$26k MRR/mo)
| Stage | Assumption | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Booked strategy calls | — | ~40/mo |
| Show rate | 60% | ~24 held |
| Close rate | 25–35% | ~6–8 → take **4** conservatively |
| Cost per booked call | $80–$200 | → ~$3.2k–$8k ad spend/mo for 40 calls |
| CAC | ~$800–$2k/client | well under the $3–6k ceiling |

At ~4 net new clients/mo (accounting for some churn), you clear **$100k/mo in ~4–5 months** — faster if close rate or retention beat plan.

## Margin protection
- Standardize the AI build (templated agents per vertical) so delivery labor stays flat as you scale.
- Onboard in cohorts; reuse playbooks; automate reporting (the Acquisition panel does this).
- Watch the two margin killers: bespoke one-off builds and heavy human hand-holding. Productize both.

## Levers to hit the goal faster
1. **Raise close rate** — guarantee + live demo (show the AI texting a lead back on the call).
2. **Raise retention** — monthly ROI report (bookings the AI produced) so the value is undeniable.
3. **Raise price** — once you have 3+ case studies, floor moves to $6k–$8k.
4. **Add setup fee** — funds ad spend, improves cash conversion.
