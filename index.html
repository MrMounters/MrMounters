Details

Worth knowing first.

How far ahead should we book?+
For weekends, proms and holidays, one to two weeks is comfortable — the party buses and the Pink Hummer book out first. Same-day and last-minute still welcome; call 480 749 4688 and we'll find something.
Can we drink on board?+
Yes, for guests 21 and over on the party buses and limousines. Several vehicles include coolers or a bar. Bring ID — your chauffeur may check, and open containers stay inside.
What's included with the vehicle?+
A professional chauffeur, fuel and the amenities listed on each vehicle — premium sound, LED lighting, and coolers or a bar where shown. Ice and basic setup are taken care of; just bring your group and your playlist.
Deposit and cancellation?+
A deposit holds your date; the balance is due before service. Cancellation terms depend on date and vehicle and are written into your quote before you commit. No surprises.
Where do you operate?+
All of metro Phoenix and the East Valley — Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale — plus wine-country and out-of-town trips on request.
How is a quote calculated?+
By vehicle, hours and date, with a minimum on peak nights. Tell us the plan and you get one figure — gratuity and any add-ons stated up front.
