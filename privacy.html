Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

Meridion AI ("we," "us," "our") builds websites, apps, and ad campaigns for local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and client portal, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or use our client portal, we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide — name, phone number, email address, business name.
  • Account information for the client and rep portals, handled through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Payment information processed directly by Stripe — we never store your full card number.
  • Call and text message metadata when you contact us via phone or SMS, handled through Twilio.
  • Booking details when you schedule a consultation through Cal.com.
  • Standard web analytics — pages visited, session behavior, and device type — collected via Microsoft Clarity.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver the services you've requested.
  • To manage your project timeline, invoices, and support requests inside the client portal.
  • To send appointment reminders, project updates, and, if you've opted in, marketing texts or emails.
  • To understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.

How We Share Information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that power our operations — Supabase (data storage), Stripe (payments), Twilio (calls/SMS), Cal.com (scheduling), Microsoft Clarity (analytics), and Vercel (hosting) — each bound by their own privacy and security obligations, and only to the extent needed to provide our services.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functionality and for analytics (Microsoft Clarity). You can decline non-essential cookies from the banner shown on your first visit, or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing texts and emails at any time, by contacting us using the details below.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account or project is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, or support obligations.
