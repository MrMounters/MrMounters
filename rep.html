// Module 00 · Brand DNA

We Are Not a Website Company.

Read that again. The fastest way to stay broke in this job is to introduce yourself as "the website guy." Website guys compete with $16/mo templates. Growth agencies compete with nobody — because the local business owner has never met one that picks up the phone.

The one sentence

Meridion AI is a United States based global digital agency — brand, websites, apps, ads, AI, and CRM under one roof, for local businesses that want their phone to ring.

The six pillars (memorize these)

  • Brand & Identity — how they look and sound everywhere.
  • Websites — custom, conversion-first, live in 5–7 days.
  • Apps — iOS, Android & web, full-stack, from $5k.
  • Ads — Google & Meta, managed for ROAS, money-back guarantee.
  • AI & Automation — voice agents that answer every call, chat & SMS that reply in seconds.
  • CRM & Lead Tracking — every lead, call, and dollar tracked in their portal.

Every first conversation should touch the full stack at least once. Not a feature dump — one sentence: "we handle everything that gets you customers."

Your 15-second elevator pitch

Copy-paste script

"I'm with Meridion AI — we're a United States based global digital agency. Websites, apps, ads, and AI that answers your phone. One team, one bill, and your phone starts ringing. What's the biggest way customers find you right now?"

Vendor vs. partner

A vendor sells a thing and disappears. A partner owns an outcome. We sell outcomes: more calls, more booked jobs, more revenue. That's why our clients pay monthly and stay — and why your residuals compound.

Remember thisNever say "I build websites." Say "I help local businesses grow." The first is a $294 conversation. The second is a $2,000/mo one.
// Module 01 · Prospecting

Find Unlimited Free Leads

You never need to pay for a lead. Every business in your city is a prospect, and most have a website quietly costing them customers. Find them free — and walk in with proof.

1. Friends & associates who own a business

Fastest yeses you'll ever get. List everyone you know who runs a business, then audit them before you reach out: load time over 3 seconds, broken links, no mobile version, no click-to-call, dead contact form, "Not secure" SSL warning, thin Google Business Profile. Free tools: PageSpeed Insights, Google's mobile check, and our Website Audit Checklist PDF.

Copy-paste opener

"Hey — was on your site and noticed the contact button doesn't load on mobile. That's costing you calls. Want me to fix it?"

2. Google Maps cold calling

An unlimited well. Search a niche + city — "attorney in New York," "roofer in Mesa," "dentist in Scottsdale." Dozens of businesses with phone numbers. No website listed? They need everything. Has one? Audit it live so you have a reason to call. Then work the list with the portal Dialer and log every conversation in your pipeline.

Copy-paste cold opener

"Hi, I help [niche] in [city] get more customers from their website — I noticed yours [specific issue]. Got 60 seconds?"

3–7. The rest of the well

  • GBPs with no website — paying for visibility with nowhere to send people. Highest intent, easiest close.
  • Facebook groups & Nextdoor — watch for "anyone know a good web person?" posts.
  • BNI, Chamber, meetups — become the marketing person in two or three rooms and referrals never stop.
  • Drive-by & service trucks — snap the number, audit that night, call tomorrow.
  • Review platforms — Yelp/Angi listings with dated sites already invest in being found.

The system

  1. Build a list (network → Maps niche/city → no-website profiles).
  2. Audit before you reach out — speed, mobile, click-to-call, SSL, reviews.
  3. Lead with the problem, not the pitch.
  4. Dial and log every deal in your pipeline.
Remember this20 audited calls a day = a pipeline that's never empty. The audit is the pitch.
// Module 02 · Selling

The Pitch & The Close

Selling this is simple: find where they're losing customers, show them, and offer to fix all of it. Questions first, pitch second, close always.

Discovery questions (ask, then shut up)

  1. "How are customers finding you right now?"
  2. "When a call comes in after hours, what happens to it?"
  3. "What's a new customer worth to you?"
  4. "If we filled your calendar without you lifting a finger, what would that be worth?"

Question 3 is the money question — once they say "$2,000 a job," every price we charge sounds small.

The 30-second pitch

Copy-paste pitch

"Most local businesses lose customers to a slow website and missed calls. We fix both — a custom site in about a week, plus an AI that answers every call and texts every lead back in seconds. You only pay for what works, and there's a money-back guarantee on ads. Want me to show you what it'd look like for your business?"

Objection kills

  • "Too expensive." → "One saved job a month covers it. The voice agent alone catches calls you're losing today."
  • "I already have a website." → "Great — is it bringing you calls? Let's audit it free and see where it's leaking customers."
  • "I need to think about it." → "Totally fair. What's the one thing you'd need to see to feel confident?"
  • "I'm too busy." → "That's exactly why this exists — we do all of it, you just answer the booked jobs."
Remember thisEvery pitch ends with a question that invites a yes: "Want me to show you what it'd look like for your business?"
// Module 03 · Expansion

Sell the Whole Stack

The website is the front door, not the sale. The real money — for the client, the agency, and your residuals — is the growth engine behind it.

Land, then expand

A $294/mo site client is a warm lead for everything else. The natural ladder:

  1. AI Voice Agent ($497/mo) — the easiest yes in the deck. Every missed call is a lost job; one saved job pays for it.
  2. Ad Campaigns ($100/wk + 10%) — now that the site converts, pour traffic on it. Money-back guarantee makes it a no-brainer.
  3. Buy-Intent Data ($697/mo) — "want to know who's shopping for you before they call anyone?" Nobody else in their market has this.

Anchor high

Present the full Growth Suite first, then let them step down. "Most clients run the full engine at around $2k/mo — but we can start with the site and the voice agent and grow from there." Starting high makes everything after it feel easy.

The money math (use their numbers)

From discovery you know what a customer is worth. Say it back: "You said a job is worth $2,000. The voice agent is $497 a month. If it saves one missed call a month, you're up 4×. It'll save more than one."

Copy-paste upsell line

"The site gets them to the door — the voice agent, ads, and tracking are what walk them through it. Want me to show you what the full engine looks like?"

Remember thisSell outcomes, anchor high, and use their own customer-value number. A site sale is the beginning of the deal, not the end.
// Module 04 · The Business

Pricing, Deals & Getting Paid

Know the numbers cold. Hesitating on price reads as doubt — and doubt kills deals.

The offer

  • Website — $294/mo (hosting, edits, support) or $3,500 one-time.
  • App development — from $5,000, scoped per project.
  • Ad campaigns — $100/wk + 10% of spend, 3-month min, money-back guarantee.
  • AI Voice Agent — from $497/mo. Answers & books every call, 24/7.
  • AI Chat & SMS — from $297/mo. Speed-to-lead + missed-call text-back.
  • Buy-Intent Data — from $697/mo. Know who's in-market before they call.

Submitting a deal

Go to Pipeline & Deals → Enter a deal. Contact, business, service, value, stage — hit Add deal, and keep the stage updated as it moves. If it's not in the pipeline, it doesn't exist (and it doesn't pay).

How you get paid

  • 15% on one-time sites ($3,500 → $525 to you).
  • First month + 5% residual for 12 months on monthly services.
  • 20% first month + setup share on high-ticket (Voice, Intent, Apps).
  • $1,000 quarterly bonus at 10 closed deals.

Do the math: ten monthly clients on a $791/mo bundle (site + voice agent) is recurring income stacking every month you keep closing.

Remember thisPipeline discipline = paycheck. Enter every deal, update every stage, and the residuals take care of themselves.
