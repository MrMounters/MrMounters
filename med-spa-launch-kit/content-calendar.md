## Recording briefs

### Founder voice

Direct, candid, operator-led. Do not open with a biography. Open with the expensive problem, show the mechanism, then give one specific action.

### UGC voice

Patient/consumer perspective without health claims. Show the speed, clarity, hospitality, and booking experience. Never imply treatment outcomes.

### LinkedIn template

1. First line: a clear contrarian business insight.
2. Three to five short lines that explain the operational consequence.
3. One specific example or metric.
4. Soft CTA: offer the Lead-Speed Audit.
