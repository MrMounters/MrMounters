# Med Spa Growth Operator - 20 / 60 / 90 Day Plan

## Days 1-20: Stop the leakage

**Objective:** establish measurement and make every paid inquiry reachable.

- Install source tracking, call tracking, CRM stages, and booked/showed attribution.
- Audit response time, missed calls, current forms, booking path, and no-show rate.
- Build instant SMS, missed-call text-back, and human escalation workflows.
- Build the approved AI concierge knowledge base and scheduling flow.
- Launch a conversion-first audit landing page and thank-you/call-booking flow.

**Success markers:** 90%+ of inquiries receive a first response within 60 seconds; every lead has a source and lifecycle stage.

## Days 21-60: Create dependable demand

**Objective:** turn the improved conversion system into consistent attended consults.

- Launch 3-5 creator/founder ad concepts with one offer at a time.
- Retarget site visitors and engaged video viewers with approved education and consultation messaging.
- Run a no-show recovery and old-lead reactivation campaign using consented first-party records.
- Publish a weekly operator dashboard: leads, speed-to-lead, booked rate, show rate, cost per attended consult, and revenue trend.
- Hold one 30-minute operator review each week to kill weak creatives and double down on winners.

**Success markers:** booked-consultation rate and show rate are measured by source; one or two winning creative angles are clear.

## Days 61-90: Scale what proves itself

**Objective:** expand only after unit economics and operational capacity are proven.

- Increase spend on the winning offer, audience, and creative combination.
- Add service-specific landing pages only where demand supports them.
- Add referral/review capture after attended appointments.
- Add retention campaigns and the next approved concierge use case.
- Build a quarterly content and promotion calendar with the clinic.

**Success markers:** cost per attended consult is predictable, staff can handle demand, and the owner can see which sources produce revenue.

## Executive dashboard

| Metric | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Median first-response time | Measures lead speed |
| Contact rate | Shows whether inquiries are reachable |
| Consult booking rate | Measures conversion |
| Show rate | Protects calendar efficiency |
| Cost per attended consultation | Primary acquisition efficiency metric |
| Consult-to-sale rate | Shows clinical/sales handoff quality |
| Revenue by source | Guides budget decisions |
