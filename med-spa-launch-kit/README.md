# Meridion Med Spa Growth Operator

## The offer

**Meridion Growth Operator** is an appointment-growth system for established med spas. It combines lead-to-speed, an AI scheduling concierge, reactivation, paid acquisition, conversion assets, and a clear operator dashboard.

### Core promise

Turn more inquiries into attended consultations without adding another full-time front-desk hire.

### Who it is for

- Med spas already spending on ads or receiving 30+ inquiries per month
- Owners with missed calls, slow follow-up, no-shows, or underused patient lists
- Teams that want better booked-consultation economics before increasing ad spend

### What we sell

1. **Lead-to-speed** - every new inquiry receives a compliant SMS/call response in under 60 seconds.
2. **AI scheduling concierge** - qualifies, answers approved operational questions, and books consultations.
3. **Reactivation** - re-engages old inquiries, no-shows, and eligible inactive patients with owner-approved campaigns.
4. **Demand engine** - UGC, founder content, paid social/search, landing pages, and conversion tracking.
5. **Operator dashboard** - source, response time, booked rate, show rate, cost per attended consult, and revenue attribution.

### Pricing guardrails

| Tier | Setup | Monthly | Best for |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Foundation | $2,500-$4,000 | $2,000-$3,000 | Lead speed, CRM, reactivation |
| Growth Operator | $5,000-$7,500 | $3,500-$5,000 | Foundation plus ads, UGC, and dashboard |
| Scale Partner | Custom | $6,000+ | Multi-location, content volume, and advisory |

Do not offer a pure pay-per-lead promise at the beginning. Use an optional performance bonus only after tracking, eligibility, and show-rate definitions are settled in writing.

## Funnel

1. Ad, referral, LinkedIn message, or outbound video.
2. Landing page: **Free Med Spa Lead-Speed Audit**.
3. Short form: location, monthly inquiries, current response time, service focus, booking link.
4. Confirmation page: choose a 15-minute Growth Operator Call.
5. Call: diagnose leakage, show the 20-60-90 plan, sell Foundation or Growth Operator.
6. Onboarding: CRM access, approved FAQs, escalation rules, brand voice, calendar, opt-in proof.

## First sales message

> Most med spas do not need more leads first. They need a faster, cleaner way to turn the leads they already pay for into attended consultations. I made a five-minute Lead-Speed Audit for clinics that want to see where inquiries are leaking. Want me to send it?

## Compliance boundaries

- The concierge schedules and routes. It does not diagnose, recommend treatment, promise outcomes, or handle emergencies.
- Never use health-condition targeting or protected-health-information lists for advertising audiences.
- Use first-party lists only when the clinic has documented permission and a lawful basis for the intended outreach.
- Require clinic approval for all offers, prices, service language, follow-up cadence, and escalation rules.
