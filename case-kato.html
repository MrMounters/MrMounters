KATO Renovation

The brief was trust before the first call.

Renovation is a visual, local, high-consideration decision. The website needed to make KATO's work feel credible quickly, explain the offer without friction, and hold up on the phone where many project searches begin.

The response was a restrained visual system that lets project imagery carry the proof. Service information, company context, and inquiry actions were organized around the questions a prospective client is likely to ask before reaching out.

  • 01

    Project-first hierarchy

    Lead with the finished work, then support it with the information needed to judge fit.

  • 02

    Clear service paths

    Give each kind of renovation work a direct place in the information structure.

  • 03

    Visible next steps

    Keep estimate and contact actions easy to find without turning every section into a sales prompt.

  • 04

    Mobile-ready delivery

    Preserve visual confidence and readable decision paths on smaller screens.

A clearer digital front door for the business.

The delivered outcome is a live website that presents the portfolio, explains the work, and makes starting a conversation straightforward across desktop and mobile.
